# Code of Conduct

Factorio Mod Studio follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space, and to people representing the project elsewhere.

In short:

* Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to modding, to open source or to English.
* Criticise ideas and code, not people.
* No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes, sexual content, or publishing other people's private information.
* Assume good intent, and accept a correction gracefully when you get one.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainer privately via the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/4a11) or, for a specific comment, use GitHub's **Report content** option.
Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn,
temporarily ban or permanently ban a participant, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant.
